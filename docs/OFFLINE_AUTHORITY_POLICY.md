# Offline authority policy — connected service decision, not activated

P4 service decision (owner-confirmed 2026-10-24, refining the 2026-10-19 online-first decision): prefer a connected service with local battle simulation and backend persistence of key progress, preserving existing reward values and unrestricted daily challenges. Do not build full offline earning, offline ticket settlement or multi-device offline merge under this scope. Short disconnection preserves the same UID's local state/candidate and conflict protection, clearly indicates unsaved/pending state and never reports backend-save success without acknowledgement. Reconnect revalidates UID/session/revision; it must not silently promote the candidate or replace a newer authoritative revision. High-value claims still require online authority. This is the implementation target, not evidence of an activated writer, changed Boot behavior or complete Cloud Save.

The current game remains locally playable; this policy defines the eventual transition and does not assert server reward writers exist. `ONLINE_REQUIRED`: paid rights, store purchases/limits, unique daily/weekly claims, Boss first-clear grants, tower milestone/chest grants, abyss first-clear/chest grants, rare relic ownership, high-value synthesis/reforge and refund/revoke. The existing local paths must remain labeled provisional until trusted writers exist.

Hero acquisition has a separately authorized bounded online source (user decision 2026-10-12; `HERO_SYSTEM_V1.md`): observed server Taipei login dates and a UID-bound chapter-one ticket/ordered-intent source, with immutable unique rights/receipts/ledger. Offline local dates, chapter flags and restored saves never award or reopen Hero rights. Ordinary chapter combat remains Client simulation; the ticket does not certify battle victory. This limited Hero source neither activates generic offline settlement nor publishes/adopts a complete character save.

`OFFLINE_LIMITED` is a retained deferred design, not an approved implementation lane: the earlier normal patrol/dungeon EXP/gold/drop/quest ticket proposal required UID/source/cap/expiry/nonce/operation identity, bounded settlement and once-only consumption. Those safety conditions are retained if a future product decision reopens the design; no ticket issuer or offline earnings are activated by retaining it. The current scope requires connectivity for settlement; local disconnected progress remains provisional and cannot authorize cloud earnings.

`OFFLINE_ALLOWED`: battle animations/VFX, read-only story, UI navigation, sound, auto-battle preference and device cache. They may reset on another phone without affecting durable entitlements.

Three historically evaluated modes: A (block high-value offline progress), B (pre-issued limited tickets), and C (accept client-only ordinary settlement). The connected-service decision retains online authority/settlement and short-disconnection protection; B is outside current construction and C does not authorize cloud settlement. Reopening offline earnings requires an explicit product decision, reward-writer inventory and abuse tests before activation. Do not introduce a daily/hourly challenge limit or change reward values under this decision. No every-action Cloud Function or local timestamp winner. Existing local play and `OFFLINE_READY` are not removed by this documentation change; any later transition uses the original Boot/Auth/Save Owners and their gates. `DATA_SECURITY_CONTRACTS.md` remains the authority ceiling and support/payment policy Owner.
